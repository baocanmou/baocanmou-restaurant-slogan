# Late Wok: ten approaches, three recommendations

**Fictional editorial demonstration.** This is an English adaptation of the Chinese stir-fry sample, not evidence of English-market testing or a real client project. Facts, principles and candidate identities are retained; wording is adapted for readability.

## Brief

Late Wok serves six individual stir-fry meals, each cooked after ordering and paired with rice, to people eating alone after a late workday. Dine-in orders run 20:30–23:00. The task is a lasting menu-front and in-store brand line. No price promotion, competitor study, customer interviews or delivery promise. The audible sizzle and hot serving are fictional supplied facts.

## Three recommendations

### 1. C09 — Late Wok. Late shifts, freshly stir-fried dinners.

Clearest brand, category and late-work occasion; less interpretation than C04 and stronger brand linkage than C05.

**Placement:** Menu front and in-store poster; keep the full brand and show 20:30–23:00 dine-in ordering hours.

**Conditions:** Only the six defined evening meals; no all-day, exclusive or delivery-state promise.

**Suggested test, not yet run:** Ask intended diners to recall the brand, food and relevant occasion; compare using the same layout.

### 2. C04 — Dinner for one still deserves a wok.

Gives solo diners a human reason to feel considered; warmer than the lead, less explicit on category.

**Placement:** Pair with Late Wok | Single-person stir-fried dinners on the menu or a solo-dining poster.

**Conditions:** Must actually offer individually cooked single-person meals; do not suggest a private chef.

**Suggested test, not yet run:** Ask what food they expect and whether the line sounds like a restaurant or kitchenware advertisement.

### 3. C05 — Hear the sizzle. Dinner arrives hot.

Cooking sound and hot serving make the food tangible; more sensory than C04, less brand-specific than the lead.

**Placement:** Pair with brand/category and an accurate dine-in food image.

**Conditions:** Do not use as a delivery-temperature guarantee or add unmeasured timing/temperature.

**Suggested test, not yet run:** Ask what food and sensation come to mind and whether they would look at the menu; stated liking is not an order.

The three recommendations are unchanged lines from the original ten. C02 remains conditional because the USP prerequisite lacks evidence. No similarity search, trademark clearance, native-speaker panel or consumer test has been conducted.
