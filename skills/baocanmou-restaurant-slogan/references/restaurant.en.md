# Restaurant-specific checks

A slogan should help a real meal choice: why this food at this restaurant, for this occasion? Start with operational facts.

| Dimension | Relevant facts |
|---|---|
| Category/product | Recognizable category, core dish, flavor, serving/eating method, temperature, texture |
| People/occasion | Commuting breakfast, work lunch, solo dinner, group meal, family visit, celebration |
| Value | Price, portion, inclusions, willingness-to-pay reason; do not assume cheap is always right |
| Kitchen/supply | Which steps occur after ordering, central preparation, on-site finishing, peak-hour reliability |
| Experience | Ordering hours, dine-in/delivery coverage, serving and packaging conditions |
| Evidence/difference | Documents, records, observable operations, competitor scope/date; separate customer statements from team guesses |
| Brand/medium | Existing proposition, desired memory, menu, packaging, poster, delivery page, video |

Use the minimum known facts; do not turn this table into a compulsory questionnaire.

| Format | Understand first | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Quick meals/stir-fry/noodles | What completes the meal, portions, ordering, actual preparation | All dishes assumed made to order; invented service times |
| Hotpot/grill | Shared preferences, broth/cooking, sharing | Only “lively”; no food reason; overconsumption encouraged |
| Regional/full-service | Cuisine memory, dishes, hospitality reason | Unsupported authenticity, ancestry or heritage |
| Tea/coffee | Ingredients, flavor, hot/iced, cup size, daily occasion | Optional added sugar described as sugar-free; refreshing described as weight loss |
| Bakery/dessert | Bake/sale timing, texture, sharing/individual use | Automatically freshly baked, molten or guilt-free |
| Raw/cold food | Handling, temperature, eating experience | Invented catch dates, live ingredients or safety guarantees |
| Delivery | Arrival texture, packaging, ordering | Promising arrival will equal the just-cooked state |
| Chains | Cross-location consistency and supply differences | One site’s capability promised at every branch |

User-provided information is not automatically independently verified. Confirmed category and operation can support copy; a metaphor must still not imply an absent process or health effect. Exclusivity, rankings, awards, nutritional claims, origins, heritage, timing and refunds need relevant evidence and scope.

“Cooked to order” does not prove never frozen, freshly cut, or no centralized preparation. Less added sugar does not establish low calories. Pleasant experience is not disease treatment.

Check the business’s local advertising and food rules when making concrete claims. [China’s official advertising-law entry](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_5474cf75173c45d6a0379730fb4e8d97.html) is a reference for Chinese use, not universal clearance. Current application depends on jurisdiction and context.

Generic warmth or value claims without restaurant information remain rough drafts. Add a meaningful category, fact, occasion or established language asset. An emotional line without a category needs explicit brand/category pairing. Tone is an editorial choice, not research unless actually supported.
