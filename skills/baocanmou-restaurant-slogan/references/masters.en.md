# Ten method cards: principles and restaurant adaptations

This is a selection of documented advertising and positioning thinkers, not a global ranking or ten universally proven laws. Each card applies one core idea. **Principles are paraphrases; the restaurant moves and review questions are BaoCanMou’s adaptations.** See [the Chinese cards](masters.md) and [the source register](sources.json) for evidence levels, including whether the material is an original excerpt, interview, institutional summary or secondary account. English wording is an editorial translation, not a quotation.

## 01 · ogilvy · David Ogilvy · Brand image

**Principle:** advertising should contribute consistently to a recognizable brand image; product, price, packaging and expression help shape that image.

**Restaurant move:** select an enduring hospitality attitude or food proposition supported by an actual restaurant fact. Check whether the line still feels like the same business on menus, packaging and staff introductions.

**Review:** what kind of restaurant should the diner remember, and what operational fact supports that impression?

**Misuse:** generic warmth, happiness or craftsmanship; equating image with a luxury tone; claiming a single sentence has already established a long-term image.

**Source:** official *Quotations of David Ogilvy*, “Brand Image & Positioning,” printed pp. 18–21. [Official collection](https://www.ogilvy.com/sites/g/files/dhpsjz106/files/pdfdocuments/O23_OGILVY75_Quotations%20of%20David%20Ogilvy_WEB_0.pdf).

## 02 · reeves · Rosser Reeves · USP

**Principle:** focus on a distinctive benefit strong enough to motivate purchase. A USP involves more than a short selling point: it needs a meaningful competitive distinction.

**Restaurant move:** identify one benefit, support for delivering it, and evidence of the competitive difference. Ingredients, process, hours, portion or service may provide material, but the benefit must matter to this meal choice.

**Review:** what does the diner gain, and what does the competitor evidence actually establish?

**Misuse:** treating fresh, tasty or cooked to order as automatically exclusive. A competitor’s silence on its menu does not prove inability. Without competitive material, write a truthful benefit direction but mark the USP application conditional.

**Source:** *Reality in Advertising*; [AAF’s institutional account of Reeves and USP](https://advertisinghalloffame.org/inductee/rosser-reeves/). The complete book was not reviewed page by page for this edition.

## 03 · hopkins · Claude Hopkins · Specific facts and testing

**Principle:** concrete information can be more persuasive than general praise, and observed response should challenge advertising judgments.

**Restaurant move:** use one confirmed operational detail that explains a dining benefit. Do not invent minutes, degrees, weights or origins merely to sound specific. Leave a proposition that can be tested.

**Review:** after adjectives are removed, what factual reason remains? Can the menu and actual kitchen support it?

**Misuse:** fake precision; sensory writing presented as a lab report; claiming a sales improvement without a test.

**Source:** *Scientific Advertising*, Chapter 7 “Being Specific” and Chapter 15 “Test Campaigns.” [Dover publisher and contents](https://store.doverpublications.com/products/9780486836058); [Library of Congress catalog](https://www.loc.gov/item/23009362/). A catalog entry is bibliographic support, not proof of full-text review.

## 04 · bernbach · Bill Bernbach · Human-centered originality

**Principle:** understand a human situation and make a product advantage felt through a relevant creative idea, with words and visuals working together where appropriate.

**Restaurant move:** identify a supported dining tension: eating alone without settling, or sharing a meal with different tastes. Let a real food or service feature resolve the tension in natural language.

**Review:** which dining situation is expressed, and does the contrast also carry a product reason?

**Misuse:** an unrelated joke; invented customer quotations; assuming any rhetorical question is the method. If a visual is necessary, state the visual condition; text alone does not complete word-image integration.

**Sources:** [DDB founder philosophy](https://istanbul.ddb.com/en/who-we-are/); [professional secondary account of ten principles](https://brandingstrategyinsider.com/2010/10/10-principles-of-advertising-bill-bernbach/). The latter is not an original speech transcript.

## 05 · burnett · Leo Burnett · Inherent product drama

**Principle:** find an engaging, credible and simply expressible drama inside the product rather than attaching an unrelated attention trick.

**Restaurant move:** inspect a real moment of cooking, serving, breaking, biting, dipping or sharing. Express appetite through an accurate sensory action. Match the food: not everything bursts, crunches or steams.

**Review:** is the interesting part the food or dining experience itself? Can the kitchen or service team demonstrate it?

**Misuse:** exaggerated processes, heat for cold food, crunch for soft food, or a camera description with no usable slogan.

**Source:** Burnett’s essay *Finally Somebody*, republished by Leo Burnett Worldwide. [Agency-published original essay](https://medium.com/leos-words-of-wisdom/finally-somebody-has-to-get-out-an-ad-f558baf9141).

## 06 · caples · John Caples · Direct headlines and response testing

**Principle:** attract useful attention with plain, direct headlines and challenge copy through actual response. A pleasing headline does not itself demonstrate selling power.

**Restaurant move:** lead with one concrete convenience or benefit of the meal. Reduce uncertainty over portions, inclusions or how to order, and propose an appropriate measure of response.

**Review:** can the diner immediately understand the benefit? Is the test measuring comprehension, selection or orders?

**Misuse:** copying the famous piano-ad structure; suspense without a payoff; treating a limited offer as a permanent brand proposition. Verify price, portion, dates, add-ons and availability when relevant.

**Sources:** *Tested Advertising Methods*; [AAF account](https://advertisinghalloffame.org/inductee/john-caples/); [book record and contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=kB7ZAAAAMAAJ). Do not attribute unchecked formulas or numbers to Caples.

## 07 · schwartz · Eugene Schwartz · Existing desire and awareness

**Principle:** identify an existing audience desire and enter at the audience’s current awareness level before directing attention to the product.

**Restaurant move:** distinguish someone solving a meal problem, comparing a category, recognizing the restaurant, or waiting for an action reason. Choose whether to lead with the desire, food or specific choice. Mark awareness assumptions when research is missing.

**Review:** what existing thought is the line meeting? Does a new customer have enough information to understand?

**Misuse:** claiming to create demand from nothing; equating hunger with every dining occasion; saying “come back” to new customers; presenting a stage assumption as research.

**Source:** *Breakthrough Advertising*, chapters on mass desire and prospect awareness. [Rights-holder introduction and contents](https://breakthroughadvertisingbook.com/lp2/). No claim of full review of the paid text.

## 08 · young · James Webb Young · New combinations

**Principle:** ideas can emerge from new combinations of existing materials through recognizing relationships, gathering, digesting and checking the material.

**Restaurant move:** connect one confirmed product detail with a concrete dining situation. Explain the relationship, then compress it into a natural line.

**Review:** where do the two elements come from? Does their connection help someone choose the meal, or merely create a puzzle?

**Misuse:** a pun replacing a reason; replacing a noun in a famous slogan; claiming an AI experienced a human subconscious incubation process. This application uses recombination and revision.

**Source:** *A Technique for Producing Ideas*, “Combining Old Elements.” [Publisher](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/mhp/product/technique-producing-ideas.html); [authorized excerpt](https://www.iberlibro.com/9780071410946/Technique-Producing-Ideas-Advertising-Age-0071410945/plp).

## 09 · ries · Al Ries · Positioning and focus

**Principle:** establish a clear intended place in the customer’s mind and narrow the focus rather than trying to own every benefit.

**Restaurant move:** choose a category or dining occasion the business can serve consistently. Connect brand and category so the line explains what this restaurant is for. Distinguish an intended position from a position already proven in customer perception.

**Review:** what position should be remembered? Could any unrelated category use the same line unchanged?

**Misuse:** an invented category customers cannot understand, unsupported leadership, or stacking fresh/fast/cheap/premium/healthy together. Ries is included as a positioning strategist, not as inventor of a personal slogan template.

**Sources:** *Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind*, coauthored with **Jack Trout**, and *Focus*. [Author profile](https://ries.com/about/alries); [official bibliography](https://ries.com/books). Do not attribute shared work or Laura Ries’s *Visual Hammer* solely to Al Ries.

## 10 · hegarty · John Hegarty · Relevant difference

**Principle:** difference must remain relevant to the brand and audience; questioning convention should reveal a useful possibility.

**Restaurant move:** identify an actually observed category convention or documented existing draft, then find a different angle the restaurant can support. Keep it relevant to choosing the meal.

**Review:** what specific convention is being questioned, what is the evidence for it, and how does the difference help the diner?

**Misuse:** shock or reversal without relevance; declaring an entire category identical without review; disparaging rivals; encouraging waste, unsafe eating or excessive drinking.

**Sources:** *Hegarty on Advertising*; [direct interview](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/advertising-going-to-have-many-futures-sir-john-hegarty-worldwide-creative-director-bartle-bogle-hegarty/articleshow/8406625.cms?from=mdr), [BBH introduction](https://www.bbh-labs.com/hegarty-on-advertising-turning-intelligence-into-magic), [Communication Arts review](https://www.commarts.com/book-reviews/hegarty-on-advertising).
