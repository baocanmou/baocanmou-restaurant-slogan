## 小红书中文稿

标题：**餐饮广告语，我做了个开源Skill**

餐饮广告语写了十几条，最后为什么选这一条？

包参谋把这个过程做成了一个开源 Skill：给餐饮资料，按十位广告与定位名家的核心方法各写一条，再用同一套标准比较，推荐三条。

它会分别解释：这句话借鉴哪个观点、跟这家店有什么关系、哪些事实必须成立、放在哪儿才合适。

比如虚构小炒店「晚点小炒」，面向下班晚、一个人吃饭的顾客，主推是：

“晚点小炒，下班晚也吃现炒。”

另两条从独食感受和食物体验出发：

“一个人吃饭，也值得起一口锅。”

“小炒滋啦响，晚饭热着上。”

完整十条、比较、使用条件和方法出处都放在 GitHub。中文、英文说明与安装包一起提供，MIT 开源。它是辅助判断的工具；实际用之前，仍要核对你店里的经营事实和顾客反应。

GitHub 搜索：`yht0912 / baocanmou-restaurant-slogan`。

## English announcement

**Ten restaurant slogans. Three reasoned recommendations. Open source.**

BaoCanMou has released Restaurant Slogans: Ten Approaches, Three Picks, an AI Skill for restaurant strategists, designers and operators.

Give it a restaurant brief. It applies one core idea from each of ten advertising and positioning thinkers, creates ten candidates, compares them and recommends three from the original set.

Each candidate explains its source-based method, restaurant facts and usage conditions. Chinese and English guides, fictional examples, visual assets and optional structural checks are included under MIT.

This is a practical editorial workflow, not an endorsement by the named authors or a promise of sales results. Start with the examples and use real restaurant facts.

Project: https://github.com/yht0912/baocanmou-restaurant-slogan
